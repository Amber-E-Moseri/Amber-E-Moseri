## 🧠 About

I build solutions to real problems — systems that fit how people operate, not the reverse. I'm obsessed with data, automation, and eliminating redundant work through systems thinking.

I started with process automation (Google Apps Script, eliminating redundant manual work). Now I build full platforms in production, owning every technical and product decision solo — architecture, schema design, RLS, feature scope, and operations.

**How I think:** I approach problems through a systems-and-processes lens — understanding workflow, dependencies, and data flow before reaching for tools. I iterate deliberately: lessons from one system (like a reporting automation built in Apps Script) inform architecture decisions in the next (how that same reporting becomes a first-class layer in a full platform, not an afterthought). I default to simple, controlled stacks over complexity, and I own the full stack end-to-end.

**🎯 Open to:** Contract work, full-time roles, or learning partnerships — particularly problems where I can own platform architecture and learn from building systems at scale.

## ⚙️ Automations & Process Optimization

**BLW Report Suite** (Google Apps Script)
Automated a 2.5-hour weekly reporting process down to under 5 minutes. Generates follow-up and first-timer reports for 6 subgroups from Elvanto data, with automatic grading, colour-coded health bands, PDF export, and email delivery. Foundation for the reporting layer later rebuilt into Nexus.

**Drive File Alert** (Google Apps Script)
Monitors Google Drive and notifies via email when files are uploaded, eliminating manual folder checks. Smart scheduling (every 15 minutes on Sundays during peak uploads, every 2 hours otherwise), and batches uploads from the same person within a 7-minute window into a single notification. Runs entirely inside Google's infrastructure — zero servers.
